# Prodigy AQA A-level PE

AQA 7582: 66 concise revision lessons across all seven theory areas, plus practical-performance/analysis guidance. The theory exams are each 105 marks and 120 minutes, worth 35% each; NEA is 90 marks and 30%.

Seven controllable teaching models cover circulation/cardiac output, muscle action, energy systems, levers, projectile flight, angular momentum and arousal. Motion starts only when requested. Every model states its assumptions; energy bars and arousal curves are illustrative rather than measured physiological predictions.

Two original 105-mark revision papers have three 35-mark sections each, saved answers, timers, print views and indicative schemes. Retrieval MCQs repeat lesson items; written contexts are new. Extended responses use original holistic best-fit levels, not official AQA grids. No official grade boundaries or endorsement are claimed. Numerical matching alone does not award all method marks.

Search, resume, the optional maths keyboard, accounts, assignments and optional provisional AI feedback use the shared platform. Self-marking remains available. NEA preparation is educational guidance: the site cannot certify practical marks, and assessed submissions must follow school authentication/assistance rules.

Rebuild data: `python scripts/build_pe_content.py` then `python scripts/build_pe_papers.py`.
Tests: `node --test tests/*.test.cjs`. Source links are in course.json and the footer. No AQA questions are reproduced.
